# Marketplace maintenance responsibilities

This is an operational checklist for EcompJr maintainers, not a record that approval or configuration has already occurred. Before publishing a Marketplace listing, record the responsible people and completion evidence in EcompJr's internal records. Do not place private contact details or legal records in this public repository.

- Have a representative with authority to bind EcompJr review and accept the Marketplace Developer Agreement. Confirm the contracting entity and rights to the source, contributions, cover image, and branding.
- Assign a primary maintainer and a backup to monitor repository issues, private security reports, privacy requests, and GitHub policy notices. Confirm that the public support contact on the listing points to the repository issue channel.
- Enable GitHub private vulnerability reporting and verify the reporting route in SECURITY.md before publication. Arrange a confidential channel for privacy requests as well.
- Review PRIVACY.md against the actual runner, build tooling, and support practices. Resolve any additional privacy notices, processing terms, transfer safeguards, or consent requirements applicable to those practices.
- Review the end-user licensing arrangement: LICENSE supplies BSD-3-Clause terms between the copyright holders and users. Preserve the complete license in the source and compiled release distribution.
- Confirm organizational acceptance of the Marketplace agreement's liability and indemnity provisions. The BSD disclaimer does not remove separate obligations to GitHub.
- Monitor dependency and security updates, release failures, and policy changes. Track compliance deadlines from GitHub notices, including any period shorter than 30 days.
- Before voluntary Marketplace withdrawal, provide GitHub the required 30 days' written notice and plan for existing users' surviving license and support obligations. Do not assume archiving the repository ends those obligations.

Public policies: [support](SUPPORT.md), [security](SECURITY.md), [privacy](PRIVACY.md), and [license](LICENSE).

Agreement reference: [GitHub Marketplace Developer Agreement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-marketplace-developer-agreement), including §§2–5, 8–11, 15, and the Data Protection Addendum. Recheck the current agreement when publishing or changing operating practices.
